<h2 align="center">🌸 About Me <sub><sup>自己紹介</sup></sub></h2>

<img align="right" src="assets/student-card.svg" alt="Student ID: Daniyar Shabdarbay, Game Designer and Unity Developer" width="300" />

**Daniyar** here: a game designer and Unity developer from Almaty who cares about how games *feel* and the systems that make them work.

I build gameplay in **Unity 6** and **C#**: combat, procedural rooms, saves, audio and localization. My pixel-art roguelite **[RULECRAWL](https://kyomutenshi.itch.io/rulecrawl)** is out now: play it in the browser, on your phone or with a gamepad.

My background is Information Systems (Game Development) at **AlmaU** with a Product Design minor, and on the side I write **Java** for the backend.

I've worked on team projects and in lead roles across design and development. Right now I'm open to **Game Designer** and **Unity Developer** roles, as well as freelance work.

<h2 align="center">🚪 Featured: RULECRAWL <sub><sup>最新作</sup></sub></h2>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://kyomutenshi.itch.io/rulecrawl">
    <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyomuTenshi/Roguelike-Prototype/main/.github/assets/banner.jpg" alt="RULECRAWL" width="100%" />
  </a>
</p>

<p align="center">
  Pixel-art roguelite where every door rewrites the rules: mechanics, light and music.<br />
  15 laws · 4 heroes · 4 modes · 84 relics · 3 endings · Unity 6 URP 2D · C#
</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://kyomutenshi.itch.io/rulecrawl"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Play_on_itch.io-E27396?style=for-the-badge&logo=itchdotio&logoColor=white" alt="Play on itch.io" /></a>
  <a href="https://github.com/KyomuTenshi/Roguelike-Prototype"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Source_code-3A4A6B?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" alt="Source code" /></a>
</p>

<details>
<summary><b>What's inside</b></summary>
<br />

- 15 laws, each with its own mechanic, post-processing, light and music
- 4 heroes and 4 modes: Run, Daily (one seed for everyone), Chaos and Endless
- 84 relics and 42 relic combos, curses, active items, a shop, an altar, a fountain and a gacha terminal
- Procedural floors rebuilt every run, plus portal worlds with their own bosses and music
- An isekai story about Adam and his sister Amelia: three corrupted saves, in-engine cutscenes and three endings
- Touch controls in the phone browser, gamepad prompts for Xbox, PlayStation and Switch, rebindable controls
- 4 languages: English, Russian, Chinese and Spanish
- **Stack:** Unity 6 (URP 2D), C#, Input System · **Platforms:** Web (desktop and mobile), Windows, macOS

</details>
